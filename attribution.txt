FREEQ Preservation Port
=======================

This is an unofficial preservation port of FREEQ, created to preserve the
game's playability and accessibility on modern computers.

FREEQ was originally created by Psychic Bunny. All original game assets,
including the story, characters, dialogue, voice performances, music, sound
recordings, sound effects, artwork, and related intellectual property, remain
the property of Psychic Bunny and their respective rights holders.

This preservation port does not claim ownership of the original FREEQ game or
its assets. It is not an official release and is not affiliated with or
endorsed by Psychic Bunny.
